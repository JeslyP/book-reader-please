    Voice

    Get better-sounding voices for free
    • iPhone / iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English. Download a voice marked Premium or Enhanced (e.g. “Ava (Premium)”, “Zoe (Premium)”), then reopen this app. Siri voices won't show up here: Apple doesn't let other apps use them, but the Premium voices sound almost as good.
    • Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices, and download a Premium voice.
    • Windows / any computer: open this app in Microsoft Edge and pick a voice with “Online (Natural)” in its name. These sound close to human.

    Uses ElevenLabs, which has very human-sounding voices and can copy your voice. You need an ElevenLabs account; usage is billed by them per character read. Your key is stored only on this device.

    Once upon a time, in a small house at the edge of a quiet town, there lived a girl who loved stories. Every evening, when the sky turned orange and the birds went home, she would sit by the window with a book in her lap. Some stories made her laugh so hard that her sides hurt. Others were so exciting that she forgot to eat her dinner. And a few were so sad that she had to stop and look at the clouds for a while. But no matter what, she always came back to the page, because she wanted to know what happened next. One day she found an old map folded inside a library book. It showed a path through the forest, across a river, and up to a hill marked with a small gold star. She wondered who had drawn it, and why they had hidden it there. The next morning, with a sandwich in her bag and the map in her pocket, she set off to find out.
